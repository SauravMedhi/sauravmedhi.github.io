Saurav Medhi

What carbon pricing and physical climate risk do to firms in a developing economy.

I am a finance PhD researcher at the Department of Management Studies, IIT Delhi, supervised by Prof. Neeru Chaudhry, with formal training in climate science and climate policy. My work sits between financial market rigour and environment and development policy, and leans on sharp econometric identification.

The current agenda asks how physical and transition climate risk reach the balance sheet: matching district level climate exposure to the places firms actually produce, and using variation in domestic carbon pricing, the social cost of carbon, and firm level emissions to identify the effect.

Working papers

2 papers
  • Working paperwith Neeru Chaudhry

    Decoding Climate Risk: Corporate Resilience under a Triad of Risks in India

    Examines how physical and transition climate risk affect corporate resilience among Indian firms. District level climate exposure is matched to firms' production locations, and identification comes from domestic carbon pricing variation, the social cost of carbon, and firm level carbon emissions.

    Presented at EFMA 2026 and ICFMCF 2026 · accepted at SWFA 2026 and AIMS 2025

  • In progresswith Neeru Chaudhry

    Climate Risk and Systemic Vulnerability of Indian Firms

    Measures firm level systemic vulnerability to climate shocks using exposure CoVaR and CRISK, then links that vulnerability back to physical and transition climate risk.
